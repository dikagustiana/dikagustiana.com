Company models

Quarterly three-statement models of IDX companies. Every figure is sourced to a published document, calculated, or an assumption you can change, and each one shows where it came from.

. Stage 1 structure: P&L, balance sheet and indirect cash flow (Q1 23 to 1H26 actual, FY totals), roll-forward schedules, Drivers, Checks and Reported. Historical inputs are transcribed only from the company's own IDX filings (financial statements, annual report, public expose); cells not yet transcribed are flagged OPEN in red and every control that depends on them reads OPEN rather than 0. Forecast columns (Stage 2) and WACC/DCF (Stage 3) follow.

Sources

Every blue cell on the Reported sheet names its document and page; the register below lists the documents. Only the company's own published documents are accepted as sources for historical figures. Press reports and screenshots are not.

Transcription coverage

    Method

    • Reported sheet: balances at period end, flows year to date as published. Discrete quarters are derived by subtraction on the statement sheets; FY columns sum the quarters.
    • Cash flow is indirect, derived from balance-sheet movements plus sourced note movements (capex, depreciation, borrowings, leases, dividends, OCI). The FX effect on cash is embedded in the movements, not plugged. K01 ties closing cash to the balance sheet.
    • Checks: every row must be 0 within the stated tolerance (±1 Rp mn, the rounding of the published statements). A row that depends on an untranscribed input reads OPEN and is counted as open, never as a pass.
    • Engine and data are separate: the formula engine and sheet definitions are generic; the DATA block holds the company's reported figures, document register, fixed-asset classes and cost components.
