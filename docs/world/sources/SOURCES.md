# Sources for the registry

Everything the generator (`scripts/world/build-registry.ts`) reads lives in this folder. The
hashes it locks to are in `citation.json`; a source file cannot change without that file changing.

| File | What it is | Who edits it |
|---|---|---|
| `idx-ic-subsektor.tsv` | The owner's table: 35 sub-sectors with their industry and sub-industry counts and a line on what each holds. The generator cross-checks every count against the classification. | The owner |
| `idx-ic-classification.tsv` | The 130 IDX-IC sub-industries: code, industry name (Indonesian), sub-industry name in Indonesian and English. Transcribed on 8 October 2026 (see below). | Nobody by hand; replaced only with the official document |
| `idx-ic-sectors.tsv` | The 12 sectors, Indonesian and English. | As above |
| `placement.tsv` | Where each sub-industry stands: zone, building family, chain stage, chain id, archetype fallback, `placedBy` and a note. Drafted from PRD Appendix A; every row `proposed` until the owner writes `owner`. The generator never flips the field. | The owner |
| `companies.tsv` | Example companies the owner names or confirms. The FSRE first slate is here. Slots this file leaves empty are filled by the generator's earliest-listed rule from the snapshot, marked `proposed`. | The owner |
| `links.tsv` | Expected links between sub-industries (supplier → customer) with a one-line rationale. All `expected`; no weights. | The owner |
| `issuers-snapshot.json` | The listed issuers per sub-industry (ticker, name, founding and IPO dates), fetched from lembarsaham.com's per-classification pages. It gives the listed counts and the earliest-listed proposals. | Regenerated by `scripts/world/fetch-issuers.py`; never by hand |
| `citation.json` | Title, publisher, URL, date and SHA-256 for the classification and the snapshot. | Updated when a source is replaced |

## The IDX-IC classification: citation and its limits

- **Official document:** "IDX Industrial Classification" (IDX-IC), PT Bursa Efek Indonesia, in force
  since 25 January 2021, replacing JASICA. The exchange publishes it on idx.co.id and as the
  Go Public sector list, `https://gopublic.idx.co.id/media/1404/daftar-sektor_web-go-public_en.pdf`
  (Indonesian: `…/1401/daftar-sektor_web-go-public_id.pdf`).
- **What could not be done here:** every request to `idx.co.id` and `gopublic.idx.co.id` from this
  environment answered HTTP 403 (curl and the fetch tool alike, 8 October 2026), so the official
  bytes could not be downloaded and hashed. The hash in `citation.json` is therefore the hash of
  the committed transcription, not of the exchange's file.
- **How the transcription was made:** the 130 codes with Indonesian and English names were read
  from two secondary pages that cite BEI as their source and agree with each other and with the
  owner's counts: `https://www.eddyelly.com/2024/01/daftar-sektor-idx-industrial.html` (codes,
  Indonesian and English names) and `https://lembarsaham.com/daftar-emiten/klasifikasi-industri-idx`
  (the full four-level hierarchy with industry names). The owner's table was the authority for
  sub-sector names.
- **Corrections made to the secondary rendering, to be checked against the official PDF:** `B142`
  "Cooper" → "Copper"; `E521` "ducation Services" → "Education Services"; `G222` "Specialize
  Business Financing" → "Specialized Business Financing"; `B153` "Diversified Forest" → "Diversified
  Forest Products"; `D113` "Supermarkets & Convenience Store" → "… Stores"; `E511` "Gaming Venue" →
  "Gaming Venues"; `I111` "Applications & Services" → "Online Applications & Services"; `A132`
  "Oil, Gas, Coal Equipment" → "Oil, Gas & Coal Equipment & Services"; `Z111` "Mutual Fund / ETFs"
  → "Mutual Funds / ETFs"; `A111` had a stray slash before "Gas". English industry names are not in
  any reachable source, so `industry.name` carries the Indonesian name.
- **Owner action (gate 1, item N):** drop the official PDF into this folder, or its URL with a byte
  hash, and the citation is updated to point at it.

## The issuer snapshot: citation and its limits

- Source: `https://lembarsaham.com/daftar-emiten/industrial/<code>/…`, one page per sub-industry
  (paginated at 20), fetched on 8 October 2026 by `scripts/world/fetch-issuers.py`. The site mirrors
  the exchange's classification of each listed company; it is a secondary source, and the exchange's
  own list on idx.co.id was unreachable (403).
- What is taken from it: the ticker, the company name, the founding date and the IPO date. Nothing
  else on those pages (prices, capitalisation, multiples) is read, kept or shown anywhere.
- How it is used: `listed.n` is the count of tickers under the code; where `companies.tsv` leaves a
  slot empty, the generator proposes the earliest-listed issuers (the longest run of filings to
  model) as `proposed`, with the rule as the reason. A code with no page gives `not yet counted`.
