## How to review

- **Part A, proposed entries.** One entry per sector per quarter, 14 in all. Each has a proposed
  `status`, a one-sentence `finding`, a `detail` of two to four sentences, and numbered sources.
  Every statement in an entry was checked against the cited document on 11 October 2026, so each
  is marked `verified`. Edit the wording inline. To drop an entry, move it to Part B.
- **Part B, proposed for deletion.** Every claim on the current page, quoted in full, with the
  verdict and the documents it was checked against. Under D6 a claim with no primary source is
  deleted, not softened. Where Part A has an entry on the same topic, that entry was written from
  the documents, not adapted from the deleted wording.
- **Part C, removed with the page structure.** Text on the current page that is not a claim
  about the world.
- **Part D, sources.** Numbered. Part A and Part B cite them as [n].
- **Part E, what this changes elsewhere,** and access notes.

**Status labels.** `advanced` / `unchanged` / `regressed` / `mixed` say what the cited documents
record for that sector in that quarter. Each entry gives the reason in one line. They were proposed
for review and approved at sign-off.

**Primary only.** Every source is the regulation, decree, plan, official release, or institutional
or company disclosure itself (PRD §B4). No news article is cited anywhere in this file.

### A1. Geothermal

#### `geothermal-2025q1`

- **quarter** 2025Q1 · **sector** geothermal · **verification** verified
- **status: advanced.** A new ministerial regulation added a geothermal price-adjustment
  rule, and a concessional loan for a geothermal expansion was signed.
- **finding:** ESDM Regulation 5/2025 on renewable power purchase agreements (signed 27 February,
  promulgated 4 March 2025) allows the price in a geothermal pre-transaction agreement to be
  adjusted in light of exploration results and the ceiling price.
- **detail:** Article 29 sets the adjustment rule, and Article 12(2) lets a geothermal plant reach
  commercial operation in stages as steam supply allows. The regulation sets no new ceiling:
  geothermal prices remain capped by Presidential Regulation 112/2022 (13 September 2022), whose

  14 January 2025 ADB signed USD 92.6 million for the 83 MW Muara Laboh expansion, including a
  USD 15 million concessional loan from the Australian Climate Finance Partnership.
- **sources:** [1] Permen ESDM 5/2025, Arts. 12(2), 29 · [2] ESDM release 018.Pers/04/SJI/2025 ·
  [3] Perpres 112/2022, Art. 6(2)(a), Annex I item 15(a), Annex II · [4] ADB news release, 14 Jan 2025

  The ESDM legal-database listings and ministry releases for Q2 contain no geothermal pricing
  instrument; the 2025 geothermal price provision found is this Q1 one.

## Part E. What this changes elsewhere, and access notes

### The PRD's example standfirst does not survive

PRD §B5 gives an example standfirst: *"Geothermal pricing moved (ESDM Permen No. __/2025, [date]);
solar procurement stalled on TKDN; JETP disbursement did not start."* The PRD says the exact wording
comes from what the sources support. They support none of the three clauses as written:

- **Geothermal pricing:** no Q2 instrument. The 2025 provision is Article 29 of Permen ESDM 5/2025
  (Q1), a price-adjustment rule inside a PPA guideline, not a new ceiling [1].
- **Solar on TKDN:** the solar minimum was lowered to 20 percent in August 2024, with an import
  relaxation to 30 June 2025 [10] [11]. No document shows solar procurement stalling on TKDN in H1
  2025.
- **JETP disbursement:** no primary document gives a disbursed total, and the partners and the
  government report approved, committed and signed financing in both quarters [21] [22] [23] [24].
  "Did not start" is not supported.

The standfirst for 2.3 will be written from the signed-off entries. A candidate that the entries
above support: *"In the first half of 2025 Indonesia ratified its ten-year power plan (RUPTL
2025–2034, 26 May) and a new edition of its green taxonomy (TKBI v2, 11 February), and JETP partners
signed new loans; geothermal tariffs and solar local-content rules stayed at their 2022 and 2024
settings."* It is a proposal, not part of sign-off on the entries.
